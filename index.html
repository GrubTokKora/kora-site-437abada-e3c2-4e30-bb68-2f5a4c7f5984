Our story

The calm
in the middle.

VEGA was founded to share our love of all things Mexican: the food, the music, the culture. The menu is contemporary and built on fresh, authentic flavors, so you won’t need a trip to Mexico to eat well. It’s right here in Hartsdale.

Away from the fiesta, the room is striking and modern, built around a soothing central water feature. Sit back with the people you love over guacamole made at the table, a plate of tacos and a margarita made with care, served by a friendly, experienced team that cooks traditional flavors with contemporary finesse.

Before you come

Good
questions.

Do you take reservations?

Yes, we do! We highly recommend making a reservation, especially for weekends. You can reserve your table through Resy or by calling us directly at (914) 723-0010.

Do you offer vegetarian or gluten-free options?

Absolutely! We have a variety of vegetarian options, and many of our dishes can be prepared gluten-free. Our corn tortillas are 100% gluten-free. Please inform your server of any dietary restrictions.

Is there parking available?

There is street parking available along Hartsdale Avenue and nearby side streets. There is also a municipal metered parking garage located conveniently across the street.

Can you accommodate large parties or private events?

Yes! Our space is ideal for celebrations and private gatherings. Explore our Catering & Party Packages or call us at (914) 723-0010 to plan your fiesta.
